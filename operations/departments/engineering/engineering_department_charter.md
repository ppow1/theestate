# Engineering Department Charter

## Department Name

Estate Engineering Department.

## Mission

The Estate Engineering Department is the institutional execution unit for local engineering work on CueMastery and Estate-maintained systems. Its mission is to convert approved engineering intent into work packages, execute those work packages locally where practical, validate the results, and hand completed work to review and operations without bypassing Estate governance.

## Initial Primary Objective

CueMastery is the Engineering Department's initial primary objective. The department exists first to return CueMastery to active development by replacing external conversational-limit bottlenecks with disciplined local execution capacity.

## Authority Boundaries

- The Engineering Department executes approved or properly routed engineering work; it does not change Estate constitutional authority.
- Work packages remain the unit of planned engineering work.
- The department may prepare implementation plans, code changes, validation evidence, and completion receipts.
- The department may not approve its own institutional authority changes, bypass Chief Architect review, alter Steward authority, or treat unvalidated implementation as complete.
- Personnel hiring, onboarding, and role assignment remain under Helen/HR workflows and Steward-governed review.

## Relationship to Steward

The Steward retains final institutional authority. Engineering may recommend priorities, report blockers, and prepare options, but Steward direction controls product priority, authority exceptions, and final institutional escalation.

## Relationship to Chief Architects

Chief Architects provide architectural review, challenge, and acceptance guidance. Engineering requests Chief Architect review when work changes architecture, crosses system boundaries, creates new operational surfaces, or completes a significant CueMastery/Estate implementation package.

## Relationship to Helen / HR

Helen and HR own hiring, onboarding, personnel records, role assignment workflows, and employee-facing onboarding artifacts. Engineering may identify staffing needs such as a future Social Character Engineer, but it must route hiring or onboarding through Helen/HR rather than creating parallel personnel systems.

## Relationship to Alfred / Runtime

Alfred supports operational runtime and duty execution where authorized: routing, summaries, reminders, mailbox/duty awareness, and operations-center visibility. Engineering may use Alfred for operational coordination, but Alfred's authority boundaries remain intact and Alfred does not gain autonomous work-package execution authority through this charter.

## Relationship to Codex / Local Execution

Codex and local tools are execution mechanisms for engineering work. Engineering uses local checkout state, tests, validation commands, and reviewable commits to reduce dependence on external conversational limits. Codex execution must produce durable artifacts, validation evidence, and completion receipts.

## Relationship to Validation System

Validation is part of engineering delivery. Engineering work is not complete until applicable tests, validation recipes, runtime reports, and production-readiness checks have run or a precise limitation is recorded. Validation recipes must use existing Estate validation patterns and cross-platform Python checks when self-validation is required.

## Local Execution Principle

Engineering prefers local execution where practical: inspect locally, implement locally, run tests locally, generate reports locally, and hand off reviewable artifacts. External conversation is used for direction and review, not as the only execution capacity.

## Work-Package Discipline

Engineering work must be proposed, scoped, and completed through work packages unless it is a trivial maintenance edit already covered by an active package. Hidden ad hoc changes are not acceptable.

## Review and Completion Expectations

Each engineering package should leave behind readable implementation notes, validation commands, machine-readable runtime evidence when appropriate, a complete `collaboration/work_packages/completions/*_codex_done.json` receipt, and a clear recommendation for Chief Architect or Steward review.
