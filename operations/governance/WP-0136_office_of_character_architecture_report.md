# WP-0136 Office of Character Architecture Report

## Summary

WP-0136 establishes the **Office of Character Architecture** and the officer title **Chief Character Architect** as canonical Estate governance terms.

- `constitution/constitution.md` now recognizes the Office of Character Architecture under Estate offices and records its responsibilities and prohibitions.
- `docs/governance/offices/Office_of_Character_Architecture.md` is the canonical office and role description record.
- `docs/operations/Document_Registry_v1.md` registers the new office record as a durable governance document.

## Path Choice

Those paths were used because this repository already separates durable governance documents under `docs/governance/`, operational reports under `operations/`, and machine-readable runtime reports under `runtime/`.

## Governance Effect

The Office is responsible for resident and worker character architecture, including identity design, traits, behavioral arcs, intelligence profiles, relationship mapping, cognition recommendations, model-capability recommendations, and implementation-pattern recommendations.

The Chief Character Architect may recommend model assignments, resident API needs, structured memory, Python-backed logic, local services, dedicated runtime state, or other technical substrates. These recommendations do not become operational access or authority until routed through Engineering, Governance, Steward approval, resident API standards, AI service/model authority paths, or another applicable Estate process.

## Boundaries

The Chief Character Architect does not grant operational authority, execute privileged actions, bypass Steward approval, override office authority boundaries, alter constitutional office powers, or unilaterally assign model/service/API access where governance requires approval.

## Recommended Next Action

Chief Architect review, then Steward ratification confirmation if desired in a future appointment or staffing package naming an occupant for the office.
