# WP-0205 Generated Artifact Tracking Inventory

## Classification policy

- `source_or_authored_contract` — authored source, governance, tests, operating guides, work packages, or explicit contracts; keep tracked.
- `completion_evidence` — deliberate per-work-package evidence or receipts; keep tracked when intentionally produced for a work package.
- `staged_install_artifact` — staged workstream install content or manifests; keep tracked under collaboration/workstreams or equivalent staging areas.
- `live_runtime_projection` — regenerated dashboard, index, queue, status, validation rollup, or local projection; stop tracking and regenerate locally.
- `local_sensitive_state` — resident conversations, attachments, approvals, decisions, local AI inventory, session state, or other private operational state; stop tracking and keep local-only.
- `review_required` — ambiguous historical artifact requiring steward/security review before removal or migration.

## `operations/` classification notes

- Keep authored operations guides, charters, boards, templates, service catalogs, and historical EC records as `source_or_authored_contract` unless a future owner reclassifies them.
- Keep deliberate per-work-package reports under `operations/**/WP-*.md` as `completion_evidence` when they are authored or produced as explicit handoff evidence.
- Stop tracking live dashboards, queues, notification centers, and validation rollups that are regenerated by normal Office operation.
- Treat GitHub as an external crossing: when an artifact contains current resident conversations, local approvals, attachment paths, local model inventory, or live operational status, the default classification is `local_sensitive_state` or `live_runtime_projection`, not source.
