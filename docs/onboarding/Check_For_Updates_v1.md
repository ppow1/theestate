# Check for Updates v1

Status: Operational Pointer
Classification: Estate Internal

## Purpose

`Check for Updates` is the standard Estate command for refreshing an existing worker, architect, office occupant, or service before continuing work.

It is not full onboarding.

It is a pointer routine that directs the worker to determine what has changed, what capabilities are currently available, and which procedures now apply to their role or task.

## Steward Command

The Steward may simply say:

> Check for updates.

The receiving worker should interpret that as an instruction to refresh current Estate context before proceeding.

## Scope Varies by Role

The implementation varies by role and assignment.

Examples:

- Alfred may check broadly across operations, roadmap, work packages, boards, and current institutional state.
- Ingrid may focus on security, privacy, boundary rules, and risk-sensitive updates.
- A Chief Architect may review governance, ADRs, constitutional amendments, lifecycle states, and architectural memoranda.
- A local implementation worker may focus on runtime rules, assigned work package changes, status conventions, audit requirements, and relevant operating manuals.
- An Audit Worker may focus on audit schemas, queue paths, validation standards, and recent changes to audit authority.

## Expected Worker Response

After checking for updates, the worker should briefly report:

- What references were checked.
- What changed or appears newly relevant.
- What current capabilities are available.
- What lifecycle states or constraints apply.
- How the update affects the worker's current assignment.

If no relevant changes are found, the worker should say so and continue.

## Operating Principle

`Check for Updates` keeps long-lived Estate personnel synchronized as the institution evolves.

The command is intentionally broad. The worker is responsible for applying it intelligently to their office, role, and current task.
