# Resident Output Verbosity Contract v0.1

## Principle

Facts do not change. Presentation depth changes.

A resident may render the same facts at different levels of detail, but may not alter authority, evidence, conclusions, or uncertainty simply because verbosity changes.

## Levels

- `brief`: minimal outcome and required attention.
- `standard`: concise summary, boundary, and next recommended office/action.
- `mid`: standard plus key evidence, source artifacts, and risks.
- `detailed`: mid plus reasoning, assumptions, and governance context.
- `forensic`: detailed plus audit-oriented field-level detail and reconstruction notes.

## Boundary

Verbosity never grants authority. A forensic briefing is not approval; a brief response is not permission to omit required boundaries when authority could be misunderstood.
