# Coordination Notice - Chief Architects #5 and #6 Execution Provider Investigations v0.1

Status: Coordination notice received by Office of Character Architecture
Office: Office of Character Architecture

## Purpose

This notice records that Chief Architect #5 and Chief Architect #6 are performing parallel architectural investigations that may constrain future Character Architecture work without redefining Estate institutional identity.

## Chief Architect #5 Scope

Chief Architect #5 is characterizing Codex CLI as an Estate execution provider.

Expected areas:

- security boundaries;
- execution interfaces;
- capability mapping;
- implementation recommendations;
- Codex integration.

## Chief Architect #6 Scope

Chief Architect #6 is characterizing ChatGPT's institutional role within the Estate.

Expected areas:

- planning responsibilities;
- connector capabilities;
- routing policy;
- relationship to Alfred;
- relationship to the Orchestrator;
- relationship to other execution providers.

## Character Architecture Interpretation

Neither investigation is expected to redefine worker identities, resident personalities, or organizational responsibilities.

The Office of Character Architecture should treat their outputs as:

- implementation constraints;
- execution-provider guidance;
- routing and interface constraints;
- infrastructure assumptions;
- possible relationship refinements.

They should not be treated as automatic changes to the Estate's institutional identity.

## Possible Character Architecture Impact

The investigations may introduce or refine execution roles, including possible Operations-related roles, provider-specific worker surfaces, routing rules, or reporting relationships.

Character definitions may require minor adjustments after CA5 and CA6 recommendations mature.

Until then, Character Architecture should continue developing:

- worker roles;
- resident responsibilities;
- resident interactions;
- organizational culture;
- public character cards;
- public resident charters;
- worker / resident distinction;
- personnel digital health.

## Shared Architectural Requirement

All future worker designs should assume that the Office of the Steward will evolve into a live operational command center.

The Office of the Steward should support visibility into:

- worker activity;
- execution state;
- resource utilization;
- human oversight;
- worker health;
- actionable decisions;
- many concurrent workers;
- multiple execution providers.

The Office of the Steward should evolve into an operational command center rather than a static dashboard.

It should present live activity, execution status, worker health, resource utilization, and actionable decisions in a way that allows the Steward to understand the state of the Estate at a glance.

## Operating Rule for Character Architecture

> Execution-provider architecture may constrain how residents and workers are implemented, routed, and observed. It does not by itself redefine who the residents are, what offices exist, or what identity boundaries govern them.

## Follow-Up Actions

Character Architecture should revisit relevant resident and worker artifacts after CA5 and CA6 produce mature recommendations.

Likely affected artifacts:

- Resident Identity Standard;
- Personnel Digital Health Standard;
- Resident Relationship Standard;
- Codex Production Contract;
- future Worker Role Standard;
- future Command Center Visualization Standard;
- future execution-provider compatibility notes in character cards.
