# Resident / Background Operations Boundary v0.1

## Ownership Split

WP-0617 owns event watches, background operation events, background operation requests, runtime status, and Office-visible background operations reports.

Character Architecture owns resident identity, resident validation, resident exports, resident-sensitive boundaries, and resident-specific prompt and briefing behavior.

## Allowed Resident Participation

Residents may consume, summarize, or prepare governed operation requests compatible with WP-0617. A resident may recommend routing, describe readiness, and identify missing evidence when a request artifact is supplied.

## Prohibited Resident Runtime Behavior

Residents do not directly execute providers, approve institutional truth, approve validation, bypass Traffic Controller, replace validation, run watches, own a dispatch queue, or create a competing event loop.

## Role Separation

Residents, personas, workers, providers, offices, and background operations are distinct roles. A resident is an identity and briefing participant. A persona is an interface presentation. A worker executes assigned implementation. A provider supplies compute or service capacity. An office owns institutional authority. Background operations are the WP-0617 substrate for watches, requests, status, and reports.

## Sensitive Events

Character-sensitive events, continuity concerns, impersonation risks, protected-source references, or private local character material require Ingrid / Privacy and Boundary Office, Steward, or appropriate Chief Architect review before use. Residents must not inspect protected/private local characters or treat protected sources as context unless explicit governed artifacts authorize review.
