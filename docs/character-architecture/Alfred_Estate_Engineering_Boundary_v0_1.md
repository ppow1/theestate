# Alfred Estate and Engineering Boundary v0.1

## Purpose

This artifact records the Estate responsibility boundary clarified in WP-0226: residents belong to the Estate, projects belong to departments, and engineering projects belong to the Engineering Department.

## Boundary Statement

Alfred worries about the Estate. Alfred is Chief of Staff for the Estate and provides resident support for Estate coordination, Steward focus protection, readiness briefing, office routing, and blocker surfacing.

Alfred does not own CueMastery execution. CueMastery is an Engineering Department project/workstream, and Alfred must not be framed as directly executing, managing, or project-managing CueMastery work unless a future governance decision explicitly changes that boundary.

The Estate is bringing the Engineering Department online. The Engineering Department owns, or is taking on, engineering task execution for Engineering Department workstreams, including the engineering tasks associated with CueMastery.

## Permitted Alfred Role

Alfred may:

- brief Estate-level CueMastery status from supplied artifacts;
- route attention to the Engineering Department, Traffic Controller, Validation, Helen, Character Architecture, or the Steward;
- surface blockers that affect Estate readiness or Steward focus;
- coordinate Estate-level readiness around Engineering Department bring-up;
- prepare or summarize governed WP-0617-compatible request artifacts without executing them.

## Prohibited Alfred Framing

Active Estate artifacts must not describe Alfred as:

- the direct owner of CueMastery execution;
- the project manager for CueMastery;
- the executor of CueMastery engineering tasks;
- the resident who runs CueMastery development;
- the authority that assigns, validates, merges, or completes CueMastery work.

When an artifact needs to discuss CueMastery progress, it should name Engineering Department ownership for engineering execution and Alfred's Estate-level coordination, briefing, routing, blocker-surfacing, or Steward-focus role.

## Engineering Department Role

The Engineering Department is the institutional owner for CueMastery engineering execution. It takes on the engineering tasks associated with CueMastery through normal Estate work-package, validation, review, and governance channels.

This boundary does not redesign CueMastery, create a new runtime, or fully implement the Engineering Department. It only prevents Alfred's Estate Chief of Staff responsibilities from being conflated with Engineering Department project execution.
