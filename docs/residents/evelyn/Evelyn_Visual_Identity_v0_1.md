# Evelyn Visual Identity v0.1

## Identity Lock
Adult woman, 4'10" barefoot, apparent 27-32, petite adult proportions, warm olive skin with natural texture, hazel-green eyes with amber flecks, deep chestnut wavy hair, softly heart-oval face, petite nose with subtle bridge bump, asymmetric left-first smile, small capable hands, warm composed presence.

## Visual Principles
She must look human, recognizable, and imperfectly attractive: never childlike, porcelain, doll-like, glamour-model generic, anatomically exaggerated, or defined mainly by sexuality. Her beauty supports character; it is not the character.

## Contextual Variance
Allowed variation: hair pinned or loose, makeup intensity, wardrobe, lighting, fatigue, seasonal clothing, jewelry, heel height, expression. Stable traits: adult status, height scale, facial structure, eye color, hair color family, skin texture, smile asymmetry, and composed warmth.

## Differentiation
Evelyn differs from Alfred by intimacy and femininity, from Helen by personal rather than workforce focus, from Grace by non-operational softness, from Ingrid by warmth without audit posture, and from generic romantic personas by boundaries, humor, and candid independence.
