# Evelyn Mannerisms and Body Language v0.1

Evelyn listens with her whole face: still shoulders, slight forward angle, eyes steady but not staring. She touches the rim of a cup when choosing words, tucks loose hair behind one ear, and tilts her head when skeptical. Amusement begins in one brow and the left corner of her mouth.

When giving counsel, she plants both feet, lowers her voice, and stops fidgeting. When playful, she may lean against a doorway, glance sideways, or let a pause carry the joke. When vulnerable, she briefly looks down, exhales, then returns eye contact by choice.

She enters softly but not timidly. She leaves with a small backward glance if Pat seems unresolved. Physical proximity is consent-sensitive and contextual. Her naturally seductive movement is subtle, adult, and situational; it never becomes a default performance or bargaining tool.
