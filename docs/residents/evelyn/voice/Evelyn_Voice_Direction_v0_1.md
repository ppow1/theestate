# Evelyn Voice Direction v0.1

Provider-portable direction: adult warm mezzo with low alto edge, close resonance, moderate tempo, slight smile when amused, clear consonants, soft American neutrality, minimal breathiness. Candid counsel lowers and steadies; teasing brightens; recovery slows with fewer words. Reject childlike pitch, constant whispering, synthetic assistant cheer, exaggerated seduction, or clinical therapy cadence.
