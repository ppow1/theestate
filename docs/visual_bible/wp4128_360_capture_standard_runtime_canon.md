# WP-4128 — Governed 360° Capture Standard Runtime Canon

## 1. Authority, purpose, and gates
This canon begins Campaign 03's strict sequential physical-comparison work. A 360 set is diagnostic evidence for evaluating one resident's body proportions, posture, silhouette, garment behavior, and cross-angle identity; it is not an animation, a beauty montage, or authority to alter physical canon. The locked physical/facial specification, measurement-review workflow, prompt and negative-prompt canons, Steward workflow, metadata/catalog system, and Campaign 02 portrait foundation are mandatory upstream evidence (WP-4108–4112 and WP-4114–4116, including recovered WP-4111). WP-4127 and Campaign 02 are merged at baseline `92e8f2f`. A missing or unresolved upstream reference blocks generation and review.

Automation establishes structural and comparison eligibility only. Only an explicit, evidenced Steward decision accepts a set. Repository/runtime completion never implies that captures exist or that any resident has been physically accepted.

## 2. Eight-angle contract
Every set contains, in clockwise subject orientation: `front` 0°, `front_left_45` 45°, `left_profile` 90°, `rear_left_45` 135°, `rear` 180°, `rear_right_45` 225°, `right_profile` 270°, and `front_right_45` 315°. These satisfy front, front-three-quarter, side, rear-three-quarter, and rear families on both sides. Subject yaw is measured clockwise from straight-on front; camera remains fixed. Nominal yaw tolerance is ±3° and may never exceed ±5°. Roll and pitch are 0° ±1°; the full body is centered vertically and horizontally within ±1% of frame. Optional intermediate angles use names such as `left_22_5` but never replace one of the eight required records.

## 3. Locked comparison controls
Within a set, every angle binds the identical controls fingerprint: identity reference and version; neutral expression; hair, makeup, facial hair, and grooming; wardrobe record/version and exact fit; normalized erect pose family with weight evenly distributed, arms relaxed enough to expose silhouette, gaze level, and no angle-specific posing; camera body/sensor, height, pitch, roll; focal length; subject distance; exposure and rendering settings; lighting rig and ratios; neutral background; marked level floor plane; framing and scale calibration. Camera rotation around the subject is forbidden: the resident/turntable rotates about a marked vertical axis without translation.

The manifest pins the canonical physical measurement record, its hash/version, canonical barefoot height in centimeters, calibration marker, pixels-per-centimeter or equivalent scale record, and crop policy. Apparent height, limb length, body width, head scale, perspective, horizon, contact shadow, and floor contact must not drift. Measurement-review variants are separately identified sets and must never masquerade as canonical measurements.

## 4. Footwear and height
`flat_foot` means both soles planted and a governed flat/barefoot-equivalent footwear record; recorded canonical height is anatomical/barefoot height. `heel` requires the same anatomical height plus shoe ID, nominal heel height, platform height, pitch, and stance compensation. Heel and flat comparisons are distinct capture sets with otherwise compatible controls. Never subtract or infer body height from image pixels or heel height. If heels materially prevent the normalized pose, queue a blocked review rather than hiding the change.

## 5. Storage, filenames, manifests, and metadata
Review assets reside at `data/visual_bibles/residents/<resident>/internal/capture_360/review/<variant>/`; accepted assets reside under the parallel `canonical` directory only after acceptance. Filename form is `<resident>__<variant>__<angle>__<footwear>__<wardrobe>__<state>__rNNN.<ext>`. IDs are lowercase snake case; revisions start at 001. Filenames are locators, never identity or approval evidence.

A resident capture manifest pins set/resident/variant IDs; measurements and height; all fixed camera, pose, wardrobe, footwear, scale, lighting, background, floor and identity records; prompt and negative-prompt template references and hashes; source references; generation workflow/version; seed; complete settings; model/LoRA/ControlNet or other adapter identifiers, versions and hashes; environment/tool receipt and record hash. Each angle records nominal/observed yaw, tolerance, controls fingerprint, lifecycle state, and—only when real bytes exist—path, SHA-256 and generation receipt. Capture and angle manifests are one governed document in v1; catalogs may index it without copying authority.

## 6. Missing angles, generation, regeneration, and revision
All eight angle records always exist. Until genuinely generated, an angle is `pending`, has no file/path/hash, and cites a queue item. A set with any pending or absent angle is incomplete, cannot be accepted, and is not comparison compatible. Failed, rejected, or inconsistent angles retain evidence and create a regeneration request naming only the defect and locked controls. Regeneration normally increments the angle and set revision while reusing the locked source/workflow/settings; any changed seed or setting is recorded. A control change creates a new whole-set revision and re-review of all eight views. Never silently patch one view, overwrite bytes, reuse an asset across angles, or declare a partial set complete.

## 7. Prompt canon
The positive template composes, in governed order: resident identity reference/version; physical measurement record/height; facial fingerprint; exact neutral expression/grooming; wardrobe/footwear; normalized pose; requested named yaw; fixed camera/lens/distance/framing/scale; fixed lighting/background/floor; quality intent; output and provenance instructions. Angle is the only intended per-view semantic delta. Required negative layers compose global anatomy/quality controls, resident restrictions, wardrobe/footwear continuity, pose/camera/scale drift, background/floor drift, identity drift, angle errors, and model-adapter syntax. Negative prompts are defect controls, not covert positive descriptions or attempts to change anatomy.

Templates are versioned in `templates/visual_bible/capture_360/`. Rendered prompt text and every component hash are stored in the generation receipt. Unknown adapter semantics block generation; random unrecorded seeds, prompt rewriting, and fabricated hashes are prohibited.

## 8. Lifecycle and Steward review
Angle and set lifecycle states are `pending`, `candidate`, `review`, `accepted`, `rejected`, and `superseded`; `incomplete` is an aggregate set state. Candidate means real evidence submitted; review means comparison is active; accepted requires all eight genuine captures plus explicit Steward approval; rejected preserves reason/evidence; superseded preserves bidirectional replacement lineage. Machine tools cannot promote to accepted.

The Steward sees a fixed-order contact sheet plus full-resolution views, measurements, scale overlay, left/right pairs, prompt/provenance diffs, validator diagnostics, prior revision, and known limitations. Review checks identity/face, expression/grooming, dimensions/proportions, posture/pose, silhouette, wardrobe fit/length, footwear/stance, camera geometry, scale, lighting/background/floor, occlusion, and all provenance. Decision evidence includes decision ID, Steward ID and authority role, time, evidence reference, notes, and requested regeneration/revision. Rejection and supersession never delete history.

## 9. Comparison compatibility and validation
Two sets are compatible only when schema and completeness validation pass; required angle order and yaw convention match; physical specification/measurement variant and anatomical height match unless the governed comparison explicitly varies them; and camera, distance, scale, pose family, lighting, background/floor, wardrobe, and footwear comparison policy match. Validators fail closed on missing/duplicate angles, tolerance errors, control-fingerprint drift, missing height/control/provenance/prompt hashes, dishonest pending assets, absent/hash-mismatched files, incomplete acceptance, or absent Steward evidence. Stable `C360-*` diagnostics are machine-readable. A pass means structurally eligible, not visually correct and not approved.

Operational diagnostics also report coverage, materialized count, completeness, and comparison compatibility. Human visual checks remain mandatory because identity and proportion drift cannot be proven from metadata alone.

## 10. Tests, anti-patterns, and completion criteria
Tests cover exact angle enumeration/order, deterministic path/name rules, pending honesty, completeness, duplicate/missing angles, orientation/tolerance, controls fingerprints, footwear/heel data, provenance and prompt hashes, files/hashes, acceptance gate and CLI integration. Fixtures use non-image bytes only for validator tests and are labelled synthetic; they are not captures.

Anti-patterns include fabricated captures/provenance; partial acceptance; filling missing views from unrelated sessions; camera orbit or angle-specific pose; changing seed/settings without receipt; using filename as identity; ungoverned crops/upscales; inferred dimensions; treating heels as added anatomical height; mirrored views; silent overwrite; automatic Steward approval; deletion of rejected/superseded evidence; and claiming physical or campaign completion from infrastructure completion.

WP-4128 runtime implementation completes when this canon, schemas, templates, deterministic runtime, per-resident pending manifests/queue, review record, validators, fixtures, tests, reports, documentation, and traceability pass. Capture production remains pending until every required resident has eight genuine, consistent views and explicit Steward acceptance. No such captures are asserted by this implementation.
