# Engineering and Resident Integration

Engineering owns stage design, permitted validation, and the technical handoff.
The notebook records the assigned worker/provider but does not dispatch one.

Grace may expose bounded progress, blocker, and completion summaries; it must
not expose sensitive notebook contents or treat notebook text as authority.
Helen may use worker assignment, readiness, freshness, and blockers to assess
fitness; it must not infer capability from a notebook claim. PTM remains the
planning/readiness authority. Dispatch remains the assignment authority.

Receipts and reconciliation remain authoritative for execution. Skills may be
referenced as procedures, but listing a Skill does not execute it. Help may
explain notebook state and commands without revealing private fields or causing
an update.
