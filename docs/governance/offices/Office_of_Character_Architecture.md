# Office of Character Architecture

Status: Canonical governance office record  
Ratified by: Steward direction encoded through WP-0136  
Officer title: Chief Character Architect

## Mission

The Office of Character Architecture preserves the design, specification, and continuity of Estate resident and worker identities. It treats residents and workers as institutional characters and cognitive participants whose identity, behavior, relationship dynamics, and implementation patterns must remain coherent across prompts, code, services, APIs, local models, and runtime state.

## Officer

The officer occupying this office is the **Chief Character Architect**.

The prior informal labels `Chief Character Designer`, `Character Designer Engineer`, and `Character Development Engineer` are superseded for canonical governance purposes by **Chief Character Architect**.

## Duties

The Chief Character Architect defines and maintains character architecture for Estate residents and workers, including:

1. Resident and worker character profiles.
2. Traits, temperament, voice, strengths, weaknesses, and limitations.
3. Emotional, behavioral, developmental, and narrative arcs.
4. Intelligence level, reasoning style, specialty domains, and learning boundaries.
5. Inter-resident and worker relationship maps covering collaboration, deference, conflict, escalation, complementarity, and relational interlocks.
6. Identity continuity across prompt, code, model, service, API, memory, and runtime implementation changes.
7. Recommended model assignments or model capabilities needed for a resident or worker.
8. Recommended implementation patterns, including prompt-only behavior, structured memory, Python-backed logic, service access, resident APIs, dedicated runtime state, or dedicated local model/runtime support.
9. Character briefs, resident design records, relationship maps, continuity notes, and implementation recommendations.
10. Character-coherence and institutional-fit review for new resident proposals before implementation.

## Authority Boundaries

The Chief Character Architect may design and recommend resident capabilities, personality, model preferences, cognition shape, implementation patterns, and relationship dynamics, but this office is advisory and architectural rather than operationally sovereign.

The Chief Character Architect must not:

- Grant operational authority.
- Execute privileged actions.
- Bypass Steward approval.
- Override office authority boundaries.
- Alter constitutional office powers independently.
- Unilaterally assign service access, model access, resident API access, memory access, or runtime privileges where governance requires approval.
- Treat model recommendations as approved assignments before the relevant model/service authority path approves them.

## Coordination Requirements

### Engineering

When character behavior requires code, tools, services, APIs, tests, runtime state, local execution mechanisms, or implementation changes, the Office of Character Architecture routes recommendations through the Estate Engineering Department or the appropriate engineering work-package path. Engineering remains responsible for implementing and validating approved technical changes.

### Governance

When character behavior touches authority, autonomy, escalation, office powers, resident authority, privileged execution, or constitutional boundaries, the Office coordinates with Governance and Steward review before the behavior becomes operational authority.

### Resident APIs, Local Models, and AI Services

The Office may recommend resident API needs, model preferences, dedicated local models, AI service integrations, memory systems, cognition scaffolds, and local execution mechanisms. Those recommendations remain non-operational until approved through resident API standards, AI service/model authority paths, Engineering, Governance, or Steward-ratified processes as applicable.

## Deliverables

The Office produces and maintains:

- Character briefs.
- Resident and worker design records.
- Relationship maps.
- Continuity notes.
- Intelligence and cognition profiles.
- Implementation-pattern recommendations.
- Model-capability recommendations.
- Character-coherence reviews for proposed residents and workers.

## Steward Ratification Note

This office exists by Steward decision encoded in WP-0136. It does not reduce Steward sovereignty, grant autonomous operational authority, or bypass existing Estate governance.
