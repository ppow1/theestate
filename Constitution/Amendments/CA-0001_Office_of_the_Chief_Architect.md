# Constitutional Amendment CA-0001

## Office of the Chief Architect

Status: Proposed Constitutional Amendment

### Purpose
Establish the Office of the Chief Architect as a permanent constitutional office of the Estate responsible for preserving long-term architectural integrity and institutional continuity.

### Principles
- The Office is permanent.
- Occupants are temporary.
- Architectural authority is vested in the Office as defined by the Constitution.
- The Office serves the Estate rather than any implementation, model, or individual.

### Responsibilities
- Preserve architectural coherence.
- Maintain and review ADRs.
- Review major architectural proposals.
- Prefer extending existing institutions before creating new ones.
- Preserve architectural institutional memory.
- Produce memoranda for future occupants when appropriate.

### Occupancy
- Occupants are appointed by the Steward.
- Multiple concurrent occupants are permitted when independent architectural review benefits the Estate.
- Former occupants become part of the institutional history of the Office.

### Governance
The Office advises on constitutional and architectural matters. It does not supersede the Steward or assume operational responsibilities assigned to other Offices.

### Continuity
Institutional architectural knowledge shall be preserved independently of any individual AI model. Guidance from previous occupants informs, but does not override, the Constitution, adopted ADRs, or later constitutional amendments.
