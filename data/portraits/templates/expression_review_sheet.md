# Resident expression-sheet review

Reviewer must record resident, sheet/asset hashes, expression slot, revision, date, and decision. Compare identity geometry, mature age, visual fingerprint, role/personality alignment, action-label match, micro-expression restraint, normalization, hidden-caption recognition, and artifacts. Decisions are `approve`, `revise`, or `reject`; only an explicit asset/hash/expression-bound Steward approval may promote a candidate. Record revision instructions, rejection codes, and predecessor/successor links. Blank form is not approval.
