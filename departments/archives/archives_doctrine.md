# Archives Doctrine
Version: 0.1
Status: Draft
Owner: Archives
Occupant: Eleanor

The Archives preserve institutional memory, provenance, lineage, revision history, and meaning.

Core principles:

1. Nothing significant is silently overwritten.
2. Every significant object shall have lineage.
3. Provenance is part of the object, not decoration.
4. Preservation does not imply universal access.
5. Every significant object has a knowledge horizon.
6. Surface simplicity may conceal deep internal history.
7. Every significant object should have a canonical address and a Palace address.
8. The Estate remembers not only what changed, but why it changed.
