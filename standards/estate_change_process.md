# Estate Change Process
Version: 0.1
Status: Draft
Owner: Archives
Occupant: Eleanor

Every significant Estate change shall receive an Estate Change number.

Format:
EC-000001

Each Estate Change shall include:
- ID
- Title
- Timestamp
- Location
- Author
- Steward approval status
- Summary
- Rationale
- Affected files
- Affected departments
- Affected residents or workers
- Classification
- Revision impact
- Ledger entry
- Devlog entry when applicable
- Decision record when applicable

Nothing significant becomes official until recorded by Archives.
