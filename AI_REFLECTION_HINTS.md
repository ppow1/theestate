Status: Canonical fast-recovery pointer for ChatGPT worker reflection and continuity.  
Classification: Estate Internal

Use this file when a long-running ChatGPT worker becomes context-congested, slow, unreliable, repeatedly loses assignment state, reopens settled decisions without evidence, or reaches a planned lifecycle checkpoint.

This is a recovery guide only. It does not replace or amend the governing specification.

## Core Rule

A reflection point preserves the officeholder, not the chat window. Renewal creates a new implementation of the same officeholder; it does not create a new employee, office, or numbered successor.

## Who Does What

- Helen owns people continuity, onboarding, care, readiness, package review, and renewed onboarding.
- Grace may supply operational degradation evidence and coordinate work impact.
- The Steward may initiate or approve reflection, define the bounded restart assignment, and judge acceptable continuity.
- The current implementation prepares the continuity artifacts and exact handoff.

## Required Layers

### Institutional onboarding layer

A bounded, complete, hashable package of current canonical materials with reading order, manifest, hashes, and onboarding validation.

### Implementation continuity layer

The current implementation must prepare:

- Reflection Point continuity package;
- Execution Handoff Addendum;
- active backlog;
- lessons learned and vital memory;
- implementation health and continuity readiness assessments;
- known uncertainties;
- exact bounded restart command.

## Minimum Completion Check

Do not call the reflection package ready until all of the following are true:

- every required artifact exists at the stated path;
- files are complete, not physically truncated;
- the onboarding package is readable and bounded;
- a manifest and hashes are present where required;
- the exact current assignment and next executable action are stated;
- inherited claims are separated from verified current evidence;
- the renewed implementation is instructed to read every listed file completely and maintain a reading ledger;
- the old implementation remains available as read-only fallback until the renewed implementation completes its first bounded assignment.
