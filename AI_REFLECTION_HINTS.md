## Determine the Request

- **Evaluation request:** assess whether the current implementation is still operationally reliable, cite evidence, identify continuity risks, and recommend renew, defer, or continue. An evaluation does not itself initiate renewal.
- **Direct reflection directive:** begin governed artifact preparation and submit the completed package for review. The worker does not approve its own renewal.

The Steward retains final authority to initiate, defer, or accept reflection where institutional judgment is required.

## Continuity Model and Deliverables

Reflection renews the implementation while preserving the office, officeholder identity, authority, obligations, and institutional continuity. It is not transcript transfer.

The two layers are both required and neither replaces the other:

1. **Institutional onboarding corpus:** office identity, authority, responsibilities, governing rules, interfaces, and durable institutional memory.
2. **Implementation continuity package:** current assignment, completed and incomplete work, blockers, evidence, next executable action, active backlog, and known uncertainties.

Using the required template, the reflecting worker produces two primary deliverables:

1. **Reflection Point Continuity Package.** Records mission and institutional state, decisions, lessons, collaboration profile, active context, reflection memo, health, and readiness.
2. **Execution Handoff.** Records the exact assignment and status, backlog, evidence references, latest blocker, bounded next action, and uncertainties.

## Evidence and Readiness Rules

Before asserting continuity facts, inspect the current repository state and applicable institutional or runtime evidence. Label verified facts, Steward-reported information, recollection, historical evidence, inference, and uncertainty distinctly. Repository evidence and institutional records outrank unverified conversational memory.

Do not call a package ready until mandatory structural validation passes. At minimum it must fail for missing or empty files, apparent mid-sentence or mid-section endings, missing required headings or template sections, unresolved placeholders, unreadable or invalidly encoded files, manifest/file mismatches, missing or mismatched required hashes, authority-ambiguous duplicate filenames, or references to nonexistent repository paths.

A structural pass establishes physical completeness and inspectability only; it is not substantive approval. Helen or the designated continuity reviewer reviews completeness and continuity sufficiency, confirms the onboarding corpus, assigns a bounded first task, and retains the old implementation as read-only fallback until successful renewal is confirmed.

State the officeholder, evaluation/direct initiation type, reason or degradation, current assignment and risk, repository and branch, and blockers. This recommendation is not authorization. If capability is unavailable, record why and use the governed manual fallback with the same artifacts, validation, Helen review, lifecycle and audit evidence.
