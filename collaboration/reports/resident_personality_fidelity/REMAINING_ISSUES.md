## Known limitations

1. **Model output remains probabilistic.** Canonical composition and acceptance checks strongly constrain behavior, but no prompt can guarantee blind recognition for every possible model output. The controlled review artifact is retained for periodic Steward review.
2. **Deterministic fallback is intentionally bounded.** When Ollama is unavailable, office-specific fallback responses preserve recognizable posture and vocabulary but cannot provide the full range of model-generated conversation.
3. **Private continuity is surface-scoped.** Conference Room behavior has identity parity with direct chat, not private-context parity: restricted relationship or memory material remains excluded by design.
4. **Live UI evidence is inherited.** This execution uses the Steward-authorized Open WebUI validation from 2026-07-19 as acceptance evidence. Automated gateway and Conference Room tests supplement it; the campaign did not reinstall or redesign Open WebUI.

These are operating characteristics, not reasons to weaken privacy, governance, lifecycle gates, or canonical authority.
