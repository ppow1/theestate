## Caption-Hidden Recognition Validation

Recognition validation is intentionally caption-hidden: generated images must contain no names, labels, captions, lower thirds, watermarks, or department text. The reviewer receives the image without its metadata and must identify every visible resident from canonical visual anchors, role posture, and department context.

Current repository result: protocol defined and queued; final recognition result remains `not_run_no_raster_generated` because this environment did not expose an image-generation tool capable of producing binary assets.
