# Campaign 03 Vertical Comparison Sheet Standard

## Sheet order
Rows are variant-aligned and columns are angle-aligned. Required rows are lower, candidate, and upper. Required columns are front, front-left 45°, left profile, rear-left 45°, rear, rear-right 45°, right profile, and front-right 45° when resolution permits; otherwise the five-column reduced review set is front, front 45°, profile, rear 45°, and rear.

## Locked controls
Only declared measurements may change. Face reference, age presentation, hairstyle, expression, camera height, focal length, camera distance, lighting, pose, wardrobe, fabric behavior, footwear, background, image scale, and crop remain fixed.

## Label block
Every sheet must label resident, review date, variant identifier, declared measurements, height, footwear and heel height, wardrobe identifier, model/workflow identifier, seed or reproducibility metadata, and review status.

## Readability gate
If the Steward cannot inspect the intended difference on one surface at normal display size, the sheet fails. Approved remedies are larger canvas, less decoration, larger figures, alignment guides, silhouette overlays, and localized crops that retain full-figure context.
