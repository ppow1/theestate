## 34.3 Escalation levels

### Level 0 — Metadata correction

Minor label, unit, or display issue.

### Level 1 — Presentation-control failure

Layout, annotation, crop, or wardrobe mismatch.

### Level 2 — Comparison-validity failure

Pose, camera, identity, or undeclared-variable drift.

### Level 3 — Decision-integrity failure

Selected or rejected evidence is missing or altered.

### Level 4 — Canonical-integrity failure

A candidate silently overwrites canon or a non-Steward actor promotes measurements.
