every asset has provenance;

# 24. Legibility Standard

A comparison is legible when the Steward can reasonably identify the intended change at normal review size.

## 24.1 Legibility factors

The system must assess:

- 

measurement spread;

-

-

- 

clothing suitability;

- 

angle usefulness;

- 

label clarity;

- 

layout;

- 

alignment;

-
