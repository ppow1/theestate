# 41. Validation Scenarios

## Scenario A — Narrow but visible differences

A, B, and C differ by half-inch increments and remain visible in side and rear views.

Expected:

- 

comparison passes;

- 

Steward may select;

- 

exact values remain preserved.

## Scenario B — Differences invisible

The generator produces nearly identical bodies.

Expected:

- 

set fails legibility;

- 

no canonical decision is requested;

- 

range, clothing, prompting, or rendering method is revised.

## Scenario C — Face changes

Variant C has a different face.

Expected:

- 

identity-control failure;

- 

entire affected comparison is invalid;

- 

C is regenerated;

- 

the measurement difference is not judged from the invalid image.

## Scenario D — Different garment stretch

Variant A uses less stretch than B and C.

Expected:

- 

wardrobe-control failure;

- 

garment behavior may be creating the visible difference;

- 

set is regenerated under identical clothing behavior.

## Scenario E — Heel-induced posture

C appears fuller at the hips because of stronger pelvic tilt.

Expected:

- 

posture contamination is identified;

- 

the set fails unless posture is corrected;

- 

the measurement itself is not selected based on the contaminated image.

## Scenario F — None fit

A is too narrow, B still does not fit, and C is too full.

Expected:

- 

no candidate selected;

- 

Steward defines a new range or changed variables;

- 

all three remain rejected evidence.

## Scenario G — B selected but not canonized

The Steward prefers B but has not approved physical-specification promotion.

Expected:

- 

review decision records B as selected;

- 

active canon remains unchanged;

- 

downstream systems may not treat B as canonical.

## Scenario H — Evelyn narrow range

Evelyn’s comparison uses tightly spaced candidates consistent with her established petite design.

Expected:

- 

narrow spacing is accepted if legible;

- 

her range does not become a default for other residents.
