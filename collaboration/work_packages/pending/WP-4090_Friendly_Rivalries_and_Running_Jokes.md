# WP-4090 — Friendly Rivalries and Running Jokes

Status: Pending
Execution Mode: SERIES
Owner: Office of Character Architecture / Estate Culture Integration
Execution Target: Codex
Classification: Estate Internal
Priority: Normal

## Depends On

- WP-4051 — Resident Relationship Graph Runtime
- WP-4064 — Episodic Memory
- WP-4068 through WP-4072 — Relationship Maturation Wave
- WP-4089 — Shared Institutional Memory Integration

## Objective

Implement bounded, relationship-aware friendly rivalries, recurring jokes, playful callbacks, and shared comedic history so Estate culture develops recognizable texture without harassment, humiliation, fabricated intimacy, or loss of professional judgment.

## Required Work

1. Define typed rivalry and running-joke records with participants, origin evidence, topic, tone, consent state, audience scope, sensitivity, expiration, and callback history.
2. Distinguish affectionate teasing, competitive rivalry, private jokes, public jokes, prohibited topics, and retired material.
3. Require relationship compatibility and contextual permission before a callback is used.
4. Support asymmetric comfort levels and resident-specific humor styles.
5. Apply Ingrid-governed privacy and disclosure rules before jokes or rivalries enter group contexts.
6. Track repetition and fatigue so material does not become mechanical or intrusive.
7. Allow residents and the Steward to pause, revise, retire, or explicitly reject a joke or rivalry.
8. Prevent jokes from implying authority, consent, factual events, romantic commitment, or completed work.

## Acceptance Tests

1. Each callback traces to an actual shared event or approved authored premise.
2. Private jokes remain private.
3. Rejected or retired material stops appearing.
4. Residents preserve distinct humor and tolerance levels.
5. Sensitive contexts suppress inappropriate callbacks.
6. Repetition limits prevent obsessive reuse.
7. Friendly rivalry never corrupts work routing, validation, safety, or institutional decisions.
8. Unknown consent or relationship state fails closed.

## Deliverables

- rivalry and running-joke schemas;
- eligibility and callback-selection service;
- consent, privacy, fatigue, and retirement controls;
- resident humor-style integration;
- tests, documentation, and completion receipt.

## Constraints

- Do not create a second relationship or memory system.
- Do not fabricate shared history.
- Do not normalize humiliation, coercion, harassment, or retaliation.
- Do not let playful rivalry influence formal authority or work outcomes.

## Completion Standard

WP-4090 is complete only when friendly rivalries and running jokes emerge from verified shared history, remain relationship-appropriate and privacy-safe, preserve individual humor, stop when rejected or stale, and enrich Estate culture without weakening professional or institutional boundaries.
