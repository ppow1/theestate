# WP-4094 — Trust and Respect Evolution Runtime

Status: Pending
Execution Mode: SERIES
Owner: Office of Character Architecture / Relationship Systems
Execution Target: Codex
Classification: Estate Internal
Priority: Immediate

## Depends On

- WP-4093 — Multi-Dimensional Resident Affinity Model
- WP-4063 through WP-4067 — Resident Memory Wave

## Objective

Implement evidence-based evolution of trust and respect so residents respond to demonstrated reliability, judgment, honesty, competence, repair, and boundary conduct over time without arbitrary scoring or personality drift.

## Required Work

1. Define typed evidence events for promises kept or broken, accurate judgment, competence, candor, confidentiality, boundary respect, support, conflict, apology, and repair.
2. Map evidence to trust and respect dimensions with source lineage, confidence, magnitude limits, and decay or persistence rules.
3. Distinguish domain-specific trust from general trust and personal respect from institutional deference.
4. Preserve negative, mixed, unknown, and recovering states.
5. Use episodic and long-term memory as evidence sources without treating unverified recollection as fact.
6. Allow residents to explain changed trust or respect in character when disclosure is appropriate.
7. Support gradual restoration after repair while preserving meaningful history.
8. Keep all changes inspectable and reversible through established review and correction paths.

## Acceptance Tests

1. Repeated reliable conduct increases relevant trust gradually.
2. One severe confidentiality breach affects the correct dimensions and remains visible.
3. Technical competence can increase professional respect without increasing personal warmth.
4. Apology without corrective action does not fully restore trust.
5. Unknown or disputed evidence cannot silently modify affinity.
6. Resident voice remains distinct while describing trust changes.
7. Trust never grants authority or bypasses privacy controls.

## Deliverables

- trust/respect evidence schema;
- evolution and recovery evaluator;
- domain-specific trust model;
- explanation and inspection surfaces;
- tests, migrations, documentation, and completion receipt.

## Constraints

- Do not gamify relationships with visible point farming.
- Do not permit arbitrary model-generated trust changes.
- Do not erase conflict history after reconciliation.
- Do not confuse respect, obedience, affection, or authority.

## Completion Standard

WP-4094 is complete only when trust and respect evolve gradually from inspectable evidence, remain domain-aware and directional, support honest damage and repair, preserve uncertainty, and never become hidden authority or a manipulable reward score.
