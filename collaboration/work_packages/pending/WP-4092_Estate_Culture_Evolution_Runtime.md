# WP-4092 — Estate Culture Evolution Runtime

Status: Pending

Priority: Normal

- WP-4088 — Estate Traditions Runtime

- WP-4053 — Dynamic Resident Preference and Personality Evolution

## Objective

Implement a governed culture-evolution layer that observes verified shared experience and proposes bounded changes to Estate customs, language, rituals, celebrations, and social patterns without allowing popularity, repetition, or model invention to rewrite institutional identity.

## Required Work

1. Define culture-signal and culture-change proposal schemas with evidence, participants, scope, confidence, privacy, risks, and review authority.
2. Detect repeated voluntary practices, new shared language, emerging customs, declining traditions, and cultural friction from canonical events and approved memory.
3. Distinguish organic behavior from authored doctrine, temporary fashion, provider artifacts, and accidental repetition.
4. Require explicit review before promoting an emerging pattern into an active Estate-wide tradition or policy-adjacent practice.
5. Preserve minority preferences, abstention, office differences, and resident individuality.
6. Support pause, experiment, adoption, revision, retirement, and rollback states.
7. Produce periodic culture reviews with source lineage and unresolved uncertainty.
8. Prevent cultural evolution from changing authority, privacy, employment, safety, or governance rules.

## Acceptance Tests

1. Repetition without verified participation does not create culture.
2. Emerging practices remain proposals until properly reviewed.
3. Private customs do not become Estate-wide norms.
4. Residents retain distinct preferences and may abstain.
5. Provider quirks and prompt artifacts are rejected as culture signals.
6. Adopted changes preserve evidence and rollback history.
7. Retired customs stop driving current behavior but remain historically visible.
8. Governance and privacy boundaries cannot be altered through culture evolution.

## Deliverables

- culture-signal and change-proposal schemas;
- evidence aggregation and classification service;
- review, experiment, adoption, retirement, and rollback workflows;
- periodic culture-review report;
- tests, documentation, and completion receipt.

## Constraints

- Do not create a second governance, preference, memory, or tradition system.
- Do not equate popularity with authority or correctness.
- Do not flatten resident differences into one Estate personality.
- Do not promote private, sexual, romantic, or restricted behavior into public culture without explicit authorized scope.

## Completion Standard

WP-4092 is complete only when Estate culture can evolve from verified shared experience through explicit, reversible review; resident and office differences remain intact; private practices remain scoped; provider artifacts cannot become doctrine; and culture growth never silently changes governance, authority, privacy, or institutional identity.
