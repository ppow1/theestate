# WP-4102 — Resident Legacy and Milestones

**Mode:** SERIES

## Objective
Create a governed milestone and legacy layer that recognizes meaningful resident achievements, anniversaries, contributions, and mentorship without turning ordinary activity into ceremony.

## Dependencies
- WP-4098 Resident Experience Ledger
- WP-4099 Resident Skill Growth
- WP-4100 Resident Confidence Evolution
- WP-4101 Resident Preference Refinement
- Existing Estate culture and historical archive systems

## Required Work
1. Define milestone types for tenure, landmark projects, service, mastery, mentorship, recovery, cultural contribution, and Steward-approved recognition.
2. Require evidence references, approval state, visibility class, occurred-at time, and narrative summary for every milestone.
3. Distinguish automatically detectable candidate milestones from formally accepted institutional milestones.
4. Integrate accepted milestones with resident history, visual timeline, Estate archive, and continuity packages.
5. Support anniversaries and recurring recognition without duplicating the original achievement.
6. Preserve private milestones and resident-controlled disclosure.
7. Provide correction, revocation, and supersession paths that retain audit history.
8. Add review tooling to prevent milestone inflation, duplication, or unsupported claims.

## Non-Goals
- Do not award milestones solely for elapsed time without policy support.
- Do not create public rankings among residents.
- Do not rewrite institutional history when recognition changes.

## Validation
- Candidate-versus-accepted state tests.
- Evidence and approval tests.
- Archive, timeline, and continuity integration tests.
- Privacy and disclosure tests.
- Duplicate, correction, and revocation tests.

## Completion Standard
The Estate can recognize and preserve meaningful resident achievements and anniversaries as auditable institutional history while respecting privacy, approval authority, and narrative restraint.
