# WP-0704 — Operational Acceptance, Graduation, and Campaign Closeout

## Campaign
CAMPAIGN-0681 — Estate Operationalization

## Purpose
Define the evidence, authority checks, graduation criteria, and closeout process required to move Estate operational capabilities from implementation into accepted ongoing operation without confusing technical availability with institutional acceptance.

## Objectives
- Establish explicit operational acceptance criteria for the capabilities delivered by CAMPAIGN-0681.
- Distinguish implemented, validated, operationally ready, accepted, graduated, and retired states.
- Require complete evidence, ownership, support, recovery, and authority coverage before graduation.
- Produce a durable campaign closeout record that preserves unresolved risks, deferred work, and successor obligations.

## Required Work
1. Define campaign-level operational acceptance and graduation criteria across service health, authority, evidence, recovery, observability, and stewardship.
2. Identify the canonical authority responsible for accepting each delivered capability and the campaign as a whole.
3. Define the evidence bundle required for acceptance, including validation results, operating procedures, ownership, recovery behavior, and auditability.
4. Define treatment of conditional acceptance, accepted exceptions, deferred scope, residual risk, and successor work packages.
5. Define graduation behavior from campaign-managed implementation into normal Estate operations.
6. Define the canonical closeout record, including final status, accepted deliverables, unresolved obligations, and links to authoritative evidence.

## Constraints
- Do not infer acceptance from deployment, uptime, implementation completion, or dashboard status.
- Do not create a new acceptance authority or bypass existing office, Engineering, Steward, or constitutional authority.
- Do not conceal unresolved risks, missing evidence, accepted exceptions, or deferred work.
- Graduation must not orphan operational ownership, support, recovery, or audit obligations.

## Deliverables
- Operational acceptance and graduation contract.
- Capability-to-accepting-authority matrix.
- Required evidence-bundle specification.
- Conditional acceptance and exception-handling rules.
- Campaign graduation and handoff procedure.
- Canonical campaign closeout record and representative acceptance tests.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Every delivered capability has an identified accepting authority and required evidence set.
- No capability is marked accepted or graduated solely because it is implemented or running.
- Residual risk, exceptions, deferred scope, and successor obligations remain explicit and traceable.
- Graduated capabilities have durable ownership, support, recovery, observability, and audit coverage.
- CAMPAIGN-0681 can be closed without losing institutional memory or creating an unowned operational burden.

## Constitutional Precedence
Existing constitutional authority, office acceptance rules, Engineering governance, and accepted campaign records remain authoritative. This work package defines the operational graduation and closeout mechanism for CAMPAIGN-0681; it does not grant acceptance authority.
