# WP-4109 — Canonical Physical Specification Standard

Status: Pending
Series: Visual Estate Initiative
Campaign: 01 — Visual Bible Foundation
Depends on: WP-4108

## Objective

Define the authoritative physical-specification model for every adult Estate resident so generated imagery, reference sheets, icons, and group scenes use one governed source of truth rather than ad hoc prompt text.

## Scope

Create a resident physical-specification schema and authoring standard covering:

- adult-status assertion and age presentation range
- canonical height, with metric and imperial values
- build class and silhouette description
- bust/chest, waist, hip, inseam, shoulder, and other approved measurements where relevant
- footwear-adjusted presentation height without altering canonical height
- proportional notes, posture, stance, and gait
- facial landmarks, eye color, hair, skin presentation, scars, marks, and other stable identifiers
- fit guidance for dress, tailoring, uniforms, and recurring wardrobe categories
- uncertainty, provisional values, and Steward-selected canonical values
- separation between stable physique and temporary presentation effects such as tailoring, foundation garments, camera angle, pose, and footwear

## Resident-Specific Rule

Do not normalize residents toward one preferred body type. Each resident receives a specification justified by her own character, role, history, and Steward direction.

Evelyn's current provisional lock must be represented without generalizing it to others:

- height: 4 ft 10 in / 147 cm maximum
- build: extremely petite adult
- provisional measurements: 33B-21-31
- silhouette: subtle hourglass
- presentation note: tailored clothing may alter apparent shape without changing canonical measurements

## Governance

- Measurements are character-design metadata, not medical facts.
- Restricted presentation details must use explicit audience scope and must not be hidden to evade HR, Security, or constitutional review.
- Canonical values require Steward approval.
- Proposed alternatives remain review variants and cannot silently replace canonical values.

## Deliverables

1. Machine-readable physical-specification schema.
2. Human-readable authoring guide.
3. Validation rules for units, ranges, provenance, and canonical/provisional state.
4. Migration guidance for existing resident profiles.
5. Evelyn example populated from current Steward decisions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every live resident can have exactly one active canonical physical specification.
- Multiple proposed variants can coexist without being mistaken for canon.
- Height and body measurements are represented in both metric and imperial units.
- Footwear and pose cannot overwrite canonical body data.
- Evelyn's 4 ft 10 in maximum height and provisional 33B-21-31 specification are preserved accurately.
- Tests reject missing provenance, contradictory active canon, and malformed measurement units.
