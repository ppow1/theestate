# WP-4096 — Protective and Supportive Behaviors

Status: Pending
Execution Mode: SERIES
Owner: Office of Character Architecture / Relationship Systems
Execution Target: Codex
Classification: Estate Internal
Priority: Immediate

## Depends On

- WP-4093 — Multi-Dimensional Resident Affinity Model
- WP-4094 — Trust and Respect Evolution Runtime
- WP-4095 — Friendship and Mentorship Runtime

## Objective

Implement bounded protective and supportive behaviors that let residents notice strain, offer help, defend appropriate boundaries, and provide practical or emotional support without becoming controlling, possessive, intrusive, or institutionally overreaching.

## Required Work

1. Define support signals including workload pressure, frustration, fatigue cues, conflict, uncertainty, isolation, and explicit requests.
2. Define resident-specific support styles such as practical assistance, concise check-ins, humor, reassurance, advocacy, quiet presence, or direct challenge.
3. Distinguish support, protection, rescue, authority, surveillance, and control.
4. Require consent or clear contextual authorization before escalating from offer to intervention.
5. Route safety, privacy, HR, operational, and authority concerns to existing institutions rather than letting residents self-appoint.
6. Preserve the Steward’s autonomy and each resident’s right to decline help.
7. Prevent affection, attraction, friendship, or protectiveness from degrading professional judgment.
8. Record significant support events as evidence without storing unnecessary private content.

## Acceptance Tests

1. A resident can notice likely overload and offer a bounded form of help.
2. Declined support is respected without sulking, retaliation, or repeated pressure.
3. Protective concern does not expose private information or bypass Ingrid.
4. A resident can defend a colleague’s boundary without claiming disciplinary authority.
5. Support behavior differs by Character Bible and relationship history.
6. Urgent risk routes to the correct established institution.
7. Attraction or affection never creates entitlement or control.

## Deliverables

- support-signal and response schemas;
- resident-specific support policy adapters;
- consent and escalation rules;
- institutional routing integrations;
- tests, diagnostics, documentation, and completion receipt.

## Constraints

- Do not implement possessiveness as care.
- Do not create covert monitoring of the Steward or residents.
- Do not let support become authority, dependency, or manipulation.
- Do not infer consent from affinity.

## Completion Standard

WP-4096 is complete only when residents can offer authentic, individualized, proportionate support and protection; refusal and autonomy remain respected; escalation follows existing institutions; and care never becomes surveillance, entitlement, control, or a substitute for professional judgment.
