# WP-0709 — Reflection and Continuity Process Adoption

## Status
Pending

## Priority
High

## Owner
Chief Architect

## Execution Lane
Codex

## Purpose
Promote the ChatGPT worker reflection and continuity process from a proposed standard to an adopted operational standard, establish `AI_REFLECTION_HINTS.md` as the canonical entry point, and correct the governing documentation based on findings from the Chief Architect renewal pilot.

## Problem Statement
The repository currently contains a functioning reflection and continuity process, but its governing documents do not consistently express its authority or operational status. `AI_REFLECTION_HINTS.md` presents the process as canonical while the master process still identifies itself as a proposed operational standard. The pilot also exposed missing structural validation requirements and unsafe reading guidance around incomplete example documents.
