# WP-4095 — Friendship and Mentorship Runtime

Status: Pending
Execution Mode: SERIES
Owner: Office of Character Architecture / Relationship Systems
Execution Target: Codex
Classification: Estate Internal
Priority: Immediate

## Depends On

- WP-4093 — Multi-Dimensional Resident Affinity Model
- WP-4094 — Trust and Respect Evolution Runtime
- WP-4088 through WP-4092 — Estate Culture Wave

## Objective

Implement friendship and mentorship as distinct, evidence-backed relationship patterns that emerge through shared history, compatibility, support, teaching, and mutual choice rather than labels assigned by the model.

## Required Work

1. Define friendship indicators including voluntary time, shared humor, reciprocal support, disclosure comfort, rituals, and remembered shared experiences.
2. Define mentorship indicators including recognized expertise, guidance requests, teaching events, feedback, progress evidence, and role boundaries.
3. Preserve asymmetry, mixed relationships, and transitions such as colleague-to-friend or mentor-to-peer.
4. Prevent mentorship from implying managerial authority, dependency, or ownership.
5. Integrate with memory, messaging, presence, traditions, and conference-room behavior.
6. Generate individualized expressions: invitations, check-ins, encouragement, challenges, jokes, and acknowledgment appropriate to each resident.
7. Support explicit boundaries, refusal, distance, and relationship cooling without punitive behavior.
8. Keep private disclosures and protected bonds scoped to authorized contexts.

## Acceptance Tests

1. Two residents become friends through accumulated reciprocal evidence rather than a direct numeric toggle.
2. A mentorship relationship remains professional even when personal warmth is high.
3. A resident may seek advice from one person while respecting another more broadly.
4. Friendship expression differs by Character Bible.
5. Cooling or boundary-setting does not trigger retaliation or task degradation.
6. Private mentorship conversations remain protected.
7. Relationship labels can be inspected and corrected through governed review.

## Deliverables

- friendship and mentorship evidence schemas;
- state evaluator and transition rules;
- individualized expression adapters;
- privacy and boundary enforcement;
- tests, documentation, migrations, and completion receipt.

## Constraints

- Do not create a social-ranking institution.
- Do not force reciprocity.
- Do not equate frequency of contact with friendship.
- Do not allow mentorship to override office roles or authority.

## Completion Standard

WP-4095 is complete only when friendship and mentorship can emerge, mature, cool, and transition from inspectable reciprocal evidence; expression remains resident-specific; boundaries and privacy are preserved; and no relationship label silently creates authority or obligation.
