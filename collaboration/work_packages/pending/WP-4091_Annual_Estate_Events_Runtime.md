# WP-4091 — Annual Estate Events Runtime

Status: Pending
Execution Mode: SERIES
Owner: Office of Character Architecture / Estate Culture Integration
Execution Target: Codex
Classification: Estate Internal
Priority: Normal

## Depends On

- WP-4088 — Estate Traditions Runtime
- WP-4089 — Shared Institutional Memory Integration
- Existing canonical calendar and scheduling systems

## Objective

Implement governed annual Estate events so recurring celebrations, commemorations, reviews, and social gatherings can be planned, experienced, archived, and revisited without creating a parallel calendar or fabricating attendance and outcomes.

## Required Work

1. Define an annual-event template with identity, purpose, recurrence, planning window, authority, participants, location, privacy, accessibility, budget reference, and archive policy.
2. Support birthdays, anniversaries, seasonal celebrations, recognition events, memorials, open houses, retreats, and Estate-specific observances.
3. Integrate with the canonical calendar, presence, traditions, locations, wardrobe, scene, gallery, and memory systems.
4. Separate invitation, RSVP, attendance, participation, and post-event evidence.
5. Permit resident-specific preparation, dress, contribution, and participation choices.
6. Generate planning and reminder artifacts without claiming the event occurred.
7. Record actual outcomes, approved images, notable moments, and unresolved gaps after the event.
8. Support cancellation, postponement, substitution, and retrospective correction.

## Acceptance Tests

1. Event recurrence follows the canonical calendar.
2. Invitations and plans are never treated as attendance evidence.
3. Cancelled and postponed events remain accurately represented.
4. Private events and guest lists remain privacy-scoped.
5. Resident participation remains individualized and optional where appropriate.
6. Archived event records identify exact sources and actual attendees.
7. Generated scenes are excluded from historical proof unless separately validated.
8. Repeated annual instances retain continuity without copying stale details.

## Deliverables

- annual-event template and occurrence schemas;
- planning and calendar integration;
- invitation, RSVP, attendance, and archive workflows;
- resident participation adapters;
- tests, documentation, and completion receipt.

## Constraints

- Do not create a second calendar, event authority, gallery, or historical archive.
- Do not fabricate attendance, gifts, speeches, images, or outcomes.
- Do not expose private guest or event information.
- Do not force identical resident participation or presentation.

## Completion Standard

WP-4091 is complete only when annual Estate events can recur through canonical scheduling, preserve individualized and privacy-safe participation, distinguish plans from actual outcomes, and create source-lined cultural history without fabricated attendance or duplicated institutions.
