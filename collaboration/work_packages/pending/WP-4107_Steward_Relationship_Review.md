# WP-4107 — Steward Relationship Review

**Mode:** SERIES
**Status:** Pending
**Owner:** Office of Chief Character Architect

## Objective
Create a bounded review and calibration process for each resident's working relationship with the Steward, preserving asymmetric history, individual expression, privacy, and the Steward's final authority.

## Dependencies
- WP-4093 through WP-4097 affinity runtime
- WP-4103 through WP-4106 Steward-awareness runtime
- WP-4067 Memory Inspection and Repair

## Requirements
1. Generate a periodic relationship review from canonical interaction, affinity, memory, adaptation, and support evidence.
2. Reviews must distinguish verified facts, resident interpretation, inference, uncertainty, and unresolved contradiction.
3. Include collaboration strengths, friction patterns, successful support patterns, rejected adaptations, and proposed calibrations.
4. Do not reduce the relationship to one score or rank residents against one another.
5. Preserve resident-specific and asymmetric perspectives; a resident's interpretation is not automatically the Steward's view.
6. Require explicit approval before persistent calibrations modify working-style, adaptation, or support policies.
7. Provide correction, redaction, and appeal paths with full audit history.
8. Protect private relationship evidence from unrelated residents and surfaces.
9. Reviews must not fabricate affection, consent, intimacy, conflict, or shared history.

## Required Artifacts
- Review schema and evidence compiler
- Classification and uncertainty renderer
- Approval/calibration workflow
- Correction, redaction, and audit services
- Privacy tests and contradiction tests
- Operator documentation

## Validation
- Reviews preserve conflicting evidence rather than flattening it.
- No single affinity score is presented as the relationship.
- Unapproved proposals do not modify runtime behavior.
- Privacy boundaries hold across residents and offices.
- Corrected evidence propagates without erasing audit history.

## Completion Standard
The Estate can periodically inspect and responsibly calibrate each resident's working relationship with the Steward using transparent evidence, protected privacy, explicit approval, and reversible changes.
