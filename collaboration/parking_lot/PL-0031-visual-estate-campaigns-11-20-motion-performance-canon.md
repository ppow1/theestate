Promote only after:

## Intended Consumer Experience

The proposed outputs are not merely folders of clips. They should support runtime experiences such as:

- Open WebUI resident instantiation with idle, listening, speaking, and greeting behavior
- FaceTime- or video-conference-style resident presence
- presentation and training scenarios
- multi-resident meetings
- reusable local video generation
- future avatar or interactive interfaces

A future Director Engine may select resident-specific performances based on conversational intent, but that engine and any new office remain separate proposals and are not authorized by this item.

## Notes

- Campaigns 11 through 20 are preserved as a structured proposal only.
- Do not begin implementation until Campaigns 01 through 10 have been reviewed in practice.
- Lessons from the Visual Canon run should materially reshape the scope before promotion.
- Avoid assuming that every proposed clip must be pre-rendered; final design may combine reusable assets, procedural animation, live generation, and state-driven performance selection.
- Preserve local/offline execution as the preferred default where practical.
