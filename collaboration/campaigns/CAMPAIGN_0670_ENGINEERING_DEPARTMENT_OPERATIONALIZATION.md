## Integration Gates

### Gate A — Baseline Freeze

WP-0670 must identify canonical assets, active seams, duplication risks, stale documents, and unresolved authority questions. No downstream package may invent a replacement for an existing mechanism discovered at this gate.

### Gate B — Intake and Execution Contract Freeze

WP-0671 and WP-0672 must agree on package identity, campaign identity, readiness, routing, provider selection, branch ownership, and evidence requirements before integration.

### Gate C — Evidence and Operations Freeze

WP-0673 and WP-0674 must align validation, acceptance, metrics, notebook, communication, and continuity records to the same campaign and work-package identifiers.

### Gate D — Operational Readiness

WP-0675 must prove the department can receive one approved campaign, decompose it, route work, execute through supported lanes, collect evidence, validate, prepare acceptance, and preserve continuity without routine Steward scheduling.
